RETURN TO SENDER
By Andrei and Kiryl

ABOUT THE GAME
Return to Sender is a top-down pixel-art action game where your enemies'
projectiles are your weapon. Explore forest ruins and a frozen wilderness,
survive the encounters, and defeat the final boss.

HOW TO PLAY
You cannot damage enemies directly. Reflect their projectiles back at them!
Aim toward an enemy and time your reflection when a projectile is nearby.
Watch the cooldown between reflections and keep moving to avoid damage.

CONTROLS
W / A / S / D       Move
Mouse               Aim
Left click / Space  Reflect a nearby projectile
Escape              Close instructions or return from difficulty selection

STARTING A GAME
Choose Play in the main menu, then select a difficulty:
Insane: 1 HP
Hard:   3 HP
Medium: 5 HP
Easy:   10 HP

The chosen difficulty sets your starting and maximum health.
Your remaining health carries over between rooms.
The How to Play button opens the in-game instructions.

PROGRESSION
Explore the first area and enter its enclosed arena.
Defeat the arena enemies, collect the key, and use the northern exit.
Follow the winding path through the frozen area to its final arena.
The arena door closes behind you. Defeat the boss to finish the game.

RUNNING A DOWNLOADED BUILD
Extract the entire game ZIP into a folder before launching the executable.
Keep all supplied game files together, including any .pck file.
You do not need the Godot editor to play an exported desktop build.

OPENING THE SOURCE PROJECT
Install a compatible Godot 4 version. The project was developed with Godot 4.7.
Import project.godot, allow assets to import, and press F5 to start at the menu.
